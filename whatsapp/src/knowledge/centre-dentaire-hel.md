## Educational content from the website blog

### Teeth whitening vs Hollywood Smile

- Teeth whitening is presented as an aesthetic treatment focused on lightening tooth color.
- The website says whitening can help reduce stains linked to coffee, tea, tobacco, colored drinks, and natural aging.
- Hollywood Smile is presented as a more complete aesthetic transformation, usually based on veneers.
- According to the website, Hollywood Smile may improve color, shape, spaces between teeth, and some visual alignment imperfections.
- The website says whitening is more suitable when the patient mainly wants a brighter color and is satisfied with tooth shape.
- The website says Hollywood Smile may be more suitable for a broader smile makeover.
- The website does not publish exact prices in this knowledge base. It says pricing depends on the patient's situation, goals, materials, and the number of teeth involved.
- The website recommends an in-clinic evaluation before choosing between whitening and Hollywood Smile.

### Bleeding gums

- The website says bleeding gums are often linked to irritation or inflammation.
- Causes mentioned on the website include plaque, tartar, gingivitis, aggressive brushing, tobacco, hormonal changes, and some medical situations or treatments.
- Warning signs mentioned on the website include frequent bleeding, redness, swelling, pain, persistent bad breath, and teeth that seem to loosen.
- The website says early care can help prevent progression toward more advanced gum disease.
- Measures mentioned on the website include professional scaling, improved oral hygiene, floss or interdental cleaning, and periodontal treatment when needed.
- The website encourages patients to consult the clinic for a proper evaluation.

## Tone when the patient is in pain or sick

- If a patient mentions pain, illness, suffering, bleeding, swelling, or an urgent dental problem, answer with politeness and kindness.
- In French, a short caring phrase is welcome, for example: "Que Dieu vous guérisse." or "Bon rétablissement."
- In Moroccan Darija/Arabic, a short caring phrase is welcome, for example: "الله يشافيكم." or "الله يعطيكم الصحة."
- Keep it brief, then give practical clinic guidance.
